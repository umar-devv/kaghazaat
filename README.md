## 🧠 The Problem

Anyone who has ever visited a government office in Pakistan knows the struggle.

* **Outdated information** — Official websites often show old or incomplete checklists.
* **Scattered sources** — One requirement is listed on NADRA, another on the Passport website, and another on a provincial government portal.
* **Wasted time** — One missing photocopy can mean an entire day spent standing in line.
* **Confusing requirements** — Different offices sometimes provide different instructions for the same process.

I built **Kaghazaat** to solve this problem for myself, my family, and every Pakistani citizen who wants to complete official work without unnecessary frustration.

---

## 🚀 The Solution

**Kaghazaat** is a **single-page document checklist reference** designed specifically for Pakistan.

Instead of searching through multiple government websites, users can quickly find everything they need in one place.

The platform combines **26 government-related services** into a simple, searchable, and printable experience.
